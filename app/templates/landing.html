Questions

How is each issue put together?
Collectors pull a few hundred recent items from about twenty outlets, Hacker News, Reddit and a handful of YouTube channels. An editor working with AI tools reads the shortlist, checks the sources, transcribes the videos that look substantive, and writes up what made the cut.
What does it cost?
Nothing.
What do you do with my email address?
Send you the newsletter. That's it. We don't sell or share it.
Can I change my mind about frequency or sections?
Yes. Every issue has a link at the bottom to change your settings or unsubscribe.
